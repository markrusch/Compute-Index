Important information

EU-CRI is a research publication. It is not investment advice and is not administered as a benchmark under EU Regulation 2016/1011; it may not be used as a reference price in financial instruments.

EU‑CRI is a price-transparency benchmark, not a settlement benchmark. It is not transaction-based, is not administered by an authorised benchmark administrator, and must not be referenced in a financial contract. Values are derived from third-party public price surfaces believed to be reliable but are not independently verified; observed prices may differ materially from prices actually obtainable. Past values are not indicative of future values. A session with too few qualifying providers is published as a gap — never back-filled, never carried forward.
